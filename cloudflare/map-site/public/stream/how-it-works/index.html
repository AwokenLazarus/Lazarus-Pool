How the money works

Not one Lightning payment per thing bought: small tickets, used up as you go.

What happens when you buy

You find a shop on Nostr and allow it in spending rules you sign. Your app connects to the seller's app and pays small tickets from your own node as you use the service. The seller counts every byte or token and signs a receipt.

What happens when you sell

You point your Stream app at something you already run on your own network: a web app, an AI model, media, miners. Your app lists it on Nostr, meters every answer, and you are paid into your own node. With the encrypted link (in progress) you need no domain, certificate or port forward.

What happens when you rent hashrate

A seller's miners work for your own DATUM Gateway, over an encrypted link. You pay for each slice of work after it arrives, counting only the shares your gateway accepted.

How the money moves

Only over Lightning, from your node to theirs, in small tickets of about 900 sat, refilled just before they run out. At most one ticket is ever at risk, and nobody holds anyone's money. The link and the relays never carry money.

What the marks mean

A plain icon is in the app today. An amber dot and dashed outline mean in progress: built and in testing, not yet released. A faded icon is designed, not yet built. Details are in the changelog and progress and status.
